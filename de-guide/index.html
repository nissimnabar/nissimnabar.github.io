For data science leaders crossing into data engineering

The Data Engineering Map

Data is produced in one place and needed in another. Data engineering is the discipline of moving it reliably, on time, in the right shape, and at a cost the business accepts. Most data science leaders know the consuming end of that journey well and the producing end much less. This guide works through the producing end one stage at a time, spends the most time on the mechanisms data scientists rarely see, and closes each section with what a leader is expected to decide.

The lifecycle this guide follows

Five stages carry data from where it is created to where it is used. Six undercurrents run beneath every stage. Subsections tagged Deep dive get extra time; those tagged Review are covered briefly because data scientists usually know them already.

  1. Generationsource systems
  2. Storagewhere data lives
  3. Ingestionbatch or stream
  4. Transformationraw to trusted
  5. Servinganalytics, ML, apps
Undercurrents: security · data management · DataOps · data architecture · orchestration · software engineering
